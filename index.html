Vision

A private production assistant for our own YouTube channels.

Vision helps our small team plan, produce and publish videos on YouTube channels that we own. It uses the YouTube Data API, YouTube Analytics API and YouTube Reporting API to upload our own videos, set titles, descriptions and thumbnails, and read performance reports for our own channels.

Vision Media Publisher (TikTok)

Vision Media Publisher is the TikTok part of Vision. A creator signs in with TikTok, picks a finished video, sees a preview, chooses who can view it and whether comments, Duet and Stitch are allowed, and sends it to TikTok, either as a draft to finish in the TikTok app or as a post. It uses TikTok Login Kit and the Content Posting API, and only for the creator accounts that sign in and approve it.

Vision is not offered to the public. It accesses only the YouTube channels whose owners have signed in and granted permission themselves.
