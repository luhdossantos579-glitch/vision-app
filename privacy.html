Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

Vision is a private tool used only to manage YouTube channels owned by its operators. This policy explains what data Vision accesses through Google APIs and the TikTok for Developers APIs, and how it is handled.

Data Vision accesses

When a channel owner signs in with Google and grants permission, Vision may access that channel's YouTube data through the YouTube Data API, YouTube Analytics API and YouTube Reporting API. This covers videos, playlists, captions, comments and channel analytics and reports.

How the data is used

The data is used only to upload and manage the owner's own videos and to analyze the owner's own channel performance. Vision does not use the data for advertising and does not build profiles of viewers.

Storage and sharing

Access tokens and reports are stored only on the operator's own computer. Vision does not sell, rent or share Google user data with any third party, and does not transfer it except as needed to call Google's own APIs.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Vision's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Vision also complies with the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

TikTok data (Vision Media Publisher)

When a creator signs in with TikTok and approves Vision Media Publisher, Vision receives the account's basic profile (open ID, display name, avatar) and permission to upload or publish videos the creator chooses. The scopes used are user.info.basic, video.upload and video.publish.

The profile is used only to show the creator which account a video goes to. Videos are sent to TikTok only after the creator presses the upload or post button. Vision does not read the creator's followers, messages or other videos, and does not use TikTok data for advertising.

TikTok access and refresh tokens are stored only on the operator's own computer and are never shared or sold. A creator can remove access at any time in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy, Security and permissions, Apps and services; the operator deletes the stored tokens on request.

Revoking access

A channel owner can revoke Vision's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. After access is revoked, the stored tokens stop working, and the operator deletes stored data on request.
